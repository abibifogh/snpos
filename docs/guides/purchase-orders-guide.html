Making a purchase order

Order what is running low, send it to the supplier, and receive it into stock.

In the admin app:
Stock → Purchase orders
Who can order Admins always. An admin ticks which roles may (Managers, Cashiers, Waiters, Cooks) under Who can make orders on the Purchase orders page. One person To let one person order, or stop them, open them on Staff and set Make purchase orders to Yes, No or As their role.
1

Start the order

Running low lists what needs ordering, already grouped by supplier and department and worked out in the packs it comes in. Press Make this order on a group.

Or press New order to start an empty one.

5

Save it

  • Save as draft keeps it to finish later.
  • Done finishes it: it becomes Ready to send.
Over the approval limit (GH₵1,000 unless changed under Features), it waits on Waiting for you until a manager or admin approves it. If it is Not approved, change it and press Done to ask again.
2

Pick the department

Choose Bistro, Bar or Shop. Only that department's stock items are offered, and the goods are received into its store and paid from its books.

Switching department clears items from the other one, after asking.

6

Send it to the supplier

Open the order, then any one of:

  • Download PDF and send the file.
  • Copy as a message, paste it into WhatsApp or a text, then press Mark as sent.
  • Email to … when the supplier has an email address. It goes within a minute.
3

Choose the supplier

Pick them from the list. Not there? Choose + A new supplier… and type their name, and a phone or email if you have them.

A manager's new supplier joins the list for next time. Anybody else's is kept on this order only.

7

Receive it when it arrives

On Stock, press Receive stock (or Record spend at the till) and pick the order under For an order. Its items fill in.

  • Change any line that came differently, say where it went and how it was paid. Not paid yet is fine.
  • Saving adds it to stock and marks the order Part received or Received.
4

Check the items

For each line: the Item, How many, Of (packs or single units) and Price each, which starts at what was last paid.

+ Add an item for more, Remove for one you don't need. Add Needed by and a Note to the supplier if useful.

✕

Cancel one that is not needed

Open it and press Cancel this order. It stays on the list, marked Cancelled, so nothing is lost.

An order's life Draft→ Waiting for approval*→ Ready to send→ Sent→ Part received→ Received

* Only orders over the approval limit, and never an admin's own.

Better suggestions

Set each item's full level, pack size and supplier under Stock. Running low tops items back up to their full level.

No double orders

An item already on an open order is left off Running low until that order arrives or is cancelled.

One department per order

The bar's drinks and the kitchen's oil from the same supplier are two orders, received into two stores.
